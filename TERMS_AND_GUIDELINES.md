## 1. Purpose of this tool

Kin Connect is an internal tool for K&M store employees to report issues, request support, and receive updates from department admins and the master admin. It is provided solely for work-related use by authorized K&M employees and administrators.

## 2. Your account

- Your login code and password are personal to you. Do not share your credentials with anyone else, including coworkers.
- You are responsible for all activity submitted under your account.
- If you believe your account has been accessed without your permission, notify your department admin or the master admin immediately.

## 3. Acceptable use

- Use this tool only to report genuine work-related issues, requests, and communications.
- Do not submit false, misleading, abusive, or offensive content — including in text, photo, video, voice, or file attachments.
- Do not attempt to access another employee's tickets, another department's data, or any admin-only feature you have not been granted.
- Do not upload content that is unlawful, discloses others' private information without consent, or violates K&M's other workplace policies.

## 4. Content you submit

- Any text, photo, video, voice recording, or file you attach to a ticket, reply, or alert is stored so that the relevant admin(s) can review and respond to it.
- Do not submit sensitive personal information (yours or others') beyond what is necessary to describe the issue.
- K&M and its administrators may use submitted content solely to investigate, respond to, and resolve the reported issue.

## 5. Notifications and communication

- By using this tool you agree to receive in-app notifications, alerts, and admin replies related to your tickets and to any broadcasts sent to you.
- Alerts (broadcasts) may be sent to you individually, as part of a group, or to all employees, at an admin's discretion.

## 6. Data retention

- Ticket, reply, and alert data is retained to support ongoing issue tracking and internal record-keeping. [Insert K&M's actual retention policy / duration here.]
- Your account and profile information are retained for as long as your account remains active, and as required by K&M's internal policies thereafter.

## 7. Changes to these terms

K&M may update these Terms and Guidelines from time to time. Continued use of the tool after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

## 8. Contact

For questions about these terms or how your data is handled, contact your department admin or the master admin.
